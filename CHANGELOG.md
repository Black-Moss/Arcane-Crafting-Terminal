Arcane Crafting Terminal 1.0.1 Update:
Fixed JEI's "+" (fill recipe) button not working with the Arcane Crafting Terminal
Filling a recipe from JEI now takes ingredients from your storage network first, then from your inventory, like Tom's Crafting Terminal
Filling an arcane recipe also tops up the crystal slots with the crystals it needs
Items already in the grid are sent back to storage before the new recipe is filled
Ingredients that can't be found are highlighted in JEI, and the rest of the recipe is still filled
